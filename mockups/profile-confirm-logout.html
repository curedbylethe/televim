TestBackend assertions this frame is the reference for

    • Exactly one of the three pane corners carries border-focused, and it is the profile at (24, 0) — the same corner the conversation lights, which is what saying “it took the conversation's slot” means. the_conversation_is_where_it_was is the other half, and a change that lit the border in both states would say nothing.
    • The label is CONFIRM and the prompt is Sign out and forget this session? (y/n) — the sibling of the quit prompt, because signing out is the other destructive thing a keypress can do here.
    • There is no hint row, and that is the rule rather than an omission. A confirmation outranks every hint, so the prompt's own sentence is what is drawn. That is also why the confirmation's own hint constant was deleted: it was width-checked by a test and shown by no one.
    • The highlight is on logout and the rest of the panel is unchanged. Confirming does not navigate, so the reader can still see what they are about to do to.
    • y is answered inside the confirmation, and the refusal replaces the prompt rather than writing under it. A confirmation outranks a flash on the same row, so a refusal written while one is up is a line the reader never sees.
