A search hit, with the cursor standing on it

A match is a bold foreground of its own colour, patched over the row rather than given a style that would replace it — so the cursor's reverse video still composes on top of it instead of erasing it. A match and a selection are the one place two roles land on the same cell: the selection is a background and the match is a foreground, which is what leaves the hit legible on top of it. Reverse video is the cursor's alone, and the two are composed for exactly this row.

TestBackend assertions this frame is the reference for

  • The matched word is match — text patched with match, so the bold and the colour both survive.
  • One cell can be selection-bg and match: background under, foreground over. Asserted by a_match_under_a_selection.
  • The cursor row carries reverse video and the match is still visible on it — the composition, not a replacement.
  • The title counts what is held ( · 1 match(es)), the status line says where the walk is (/bench — match 1 of 2). One is the messages that can be marked; the other is the reader's position in all of them.
  • A local search says 2 loaded and never a final count, because a local list is not an answer.
