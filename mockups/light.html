The reference frame, on a light terminal

The reader's terminal may be light, and the palette is specified for both. The four mode labels are the rows that have to survive it, and all four take one ink here: each is painted in #0d1117. Two of their backgrounds were too dark for it — mode-normal at #3a6a99 was 3.34:1 and mode-confirm at #c0454e was 3.79:1 — so those two were lightened until the ink cleared 4.5:1, which is why there is no second foreground. selection-bg is #6b4bab on both terminals and its text is #d7dce5 on both: a selection's own ink follows the selection rather than the terminal, because #1d2128 on it is 2.48:1.

TestBackend assertions this frame is the reference for

  • All four mode labels are legible on this background, each in the one ink #0d1117, which is the property the v2 palette is chosen for.
  • border is #b0b5c0 and border-focused is #1a7f8c here — the same role, the same two values of emphasis, re-specified rather than approximated.
  • text is #1d2128 and text-dim is #6b7280.
  • The same two composition rules hold: reverse video is the cursor's alone, and a selection is a background with its own ink, because the text on it follows the selection rather than the terminal.
  • A 16-colour terminal approximates the truecolour escapes, and the v2 values are desaturated so that approximation is survivable. It degrades; it does not break.
